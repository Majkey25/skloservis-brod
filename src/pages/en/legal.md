## Enquiries and quotations

This website presents products and services. Sending an enquiry does not itself place an order or conclude a contract. The specification, price, timing and project terms must be agreed individually.

This website has no online shop or payment gateway. This does not remove consumer rights when a contract is subsequently concluded remotely or away from business premises.

## Complaints and project questions

Contact us about a complaint or project question using the email, phone number or workshop address above. Provide enough information to identify the project and describe the defect. A photograph can help, but it is not the only way to raise a complaint. Applicable law governs rights relating to defects; made-to-measure work does not itself remove those rights.

## Consumer alternative dispute resolution

If a consumer dispute cannot be resolved directly with us, you can contact **Česká obchodní inspekce, the Czech Trade Inspection Authority**, the relevant consumer alternative dispute resolution body. See the [ČOI ADR information and procedure](https://coi.gov.cz/informace-o-adr/).
